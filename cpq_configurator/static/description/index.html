Product Configurator, Configure Price Quote (CPQ) for Odoo

Turn any product into a configurable one. Add your own attributes, let the salesperson configure the product on the quotation, and get the price calculated live. Works for any made-to-measure or configure-to-order business: joinery, glass, sheet metal, furniture, blinds, signage, packaging and more.

What you get, for free

  • Typed attributes: selection list, numeric, yes/no.
  • A configurator popup on each sale order line, with a smart button.
  • Option surcharges on list choices and yes/no options.
  • Per-unit rates on numeric attributes (value multiplied by a rate).
  • Named pricing formulas that combine your attributes, with a safe expression editor (no programming).
  • Live price that recomputes as the salesperson changes the configuration, with a clear price breakdown.
  • A frozen snapshot of the configuration saved on the quotation line.

How the price is built

The unit price is transparent and fully documented:

Price = base price
    + option surcharges
    + numeric value x rate
    + pricing formulas

A formula can combine several attributes, for example width * height * rate_material. Surface, volume, weight and perimeter are just a few of the formulas you can write.

Build your configurable product

Add attributes, set a rate or write a formula, and see the price formula summarized at a glance.

Configure and price on the quotation

The salesperson opens the configurator from the order line, picks the options, and the total updates live with a full breakdown.
